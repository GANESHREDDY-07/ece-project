Project measurement logic

The load cell measures the increase in the waste container's weight during a student's deposit. The website stores that event against a verified student ID, meal and timestamp. A dashboard can then total waste per student, per meal and per day.

Important: This demo uses sample values. Real totals will come from Arduino/HX711 readings, not from the illustrative weekly chart.
